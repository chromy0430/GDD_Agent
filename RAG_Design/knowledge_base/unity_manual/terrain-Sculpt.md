# Advanced sculpting tools
**Note:** These tools are only available in the [Terrain Tools package](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.terrain-tools@latest/).
The advanced sculpting tools automate some complex sculpting tasks. The tools are:
| **Tool** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| [**Add noise to your terrain**](terrain-Sculpt-Noise-Landing.html) | Adds noise or randomness to the **terrain**The landscape in your scene. A Terrain GameObject adds a large flat plane to your scene and you can use the Terrain’s Inspector window to create a detailed landscape. [More info](terrain-UsingTerrains.html) See in [Glossary](Glossary.html#Terrain). |
| [**Build a terrace**](terrain-Sculpt-Terrace.html) | Creates a stepped effect on the terrain. |
| [**Clone a part of the terrain**](terrain-Sculpt-Clone.html) | Copies a section of terrain and pastes it elsewhere. |
| [**Build a bridge between two points**](terrain-Sculpt-Bridge.html) | Creates a bridge between two areas of terrain. |
