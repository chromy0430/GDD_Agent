# Transcode your video files
To enable transcoding for your video files, you need to change the settings in the Video Clip Importer settings. Follow these steps:
## Access and configure the transcoding settings
To transcode your video files:
1. Select your [video clip asset](video-clips-use.html). The **Inspector**A Unity window that displays information about the currently selected GameObject, asset or project settings, allowing you to inspect and edit the values. [More info](UsingTheInspector.html)
   See in [Glossary](Glossary.html#Inspector) window shows the **Video Clip Import Settings**.
2. Enable **Transcode**.
3. Configure the transcoding settings in the [Video Clip Import Settings](class-VideoClip.html#transcode-options).
4. Once you’re satisfied with your settings, select **Apply**.
Unity transcodes your video file with your new settings.
