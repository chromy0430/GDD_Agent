# Change the appearance and position of an overlay
Change how overlays appear in the **Scene**A Scene contains the environments and menus of your game. Think of each unique Scene file as a unique level. In each Scene, you place your environments, obstacles, and decorations, essentially designing and building your game in pieces. [More info](CreatingScenes.html)
See in [Glossary](Glossary.html#Scene) view.
