# Velocity over Lifetime module reference
The **Velocity over Lifetime** module allows you to control the velocity of **particles**A small, simple image or mesh that is emitted by a particle system. A particle system can display and move particles in great numbers to represent a fluid or amorphous entity. The effect of all the particles together creates the impression of the complete entity, such as smoke. [More info](class-ParticleSystem.html)
See in [Glossary](Glossary.html#particle) over their lifetime.
## Properties
For some properties in this section, you can use different modes to set their value. For information on the modes you can use, refer to [Vary Particle System properties over time](varying-particle-system-properties-over-time.html).
| **Property** | **Function** |
| --- | --- |
| **Linear X, Y, Z** | Linear velocity of particles in the X, Y and Z axes. |
| **Space** | Specifies whether the **Linear X, Y, Z** axes refer to local or world space. |
| **Orbital X, Y, Z** | Orbital velocity of particles around the X, Y and Z axes. |
| **Offset X, Y, Z** | The position of the center of orbit, for orbiting particles. |
| **Radial** | Radial velocity of particles away from/towards the center position. |
| **Speed Modifier** | Applies a multiplier to the speed of particles, along/around their current direction of travel. |
