# Particle movement
Resources for configuring **particle**A small, simple image or mesh that is emitted by a particle system. A particle system can display and move particles in great numbers to represent a fluid or amorphous entity. The effect of all the particles together creates the impression of the complete entity, such as smoke. [More info](class-ParticleSystem.html)
